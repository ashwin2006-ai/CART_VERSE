## 🎓 Common Tasks

### ➕ Add a New Product
1. Click **📦 Products**
2. Click **+ Add New Product**
3. Fill in details (Name, Price, Stock, Images)
4. Click **Save Product**

### 📦 Update Stock Levels
1. Click **📚 Inventory**
2. Find product and click to edit
3. Update stock quantity
4. Set low-stock alert threshold
5. Save changes

### ✅ Process an Order
1. Dashboard shows new order
2. Click **🛒 Orders**
3. Find and click the order
4. Update status: Pending → Confirmed → Shipped
5. Add tracking number
6. Customer gets notification

### 💰 Create a Promotion
1. Click **🎟️ Coupons**
2. Click **+ Add Coupon**
3. Enter code, discount %, expiry date
4. Click **Create**
5. Share code with customers

### 💬 Respond to Review
1. Click **⭐ Reviews**
2. Find negative review
3. Click **Reply**
4. Type professional response
5. Click **Post Reply**

## ⚠️ Important Alerts

### Low Stock Warning (Yellow)
- Shows when product has 5 or fewer units
- Action: Reorder from supplier
- Click alert to manage inventory

### Out of Stock (Red)
- Shows when product has 0 units
- Action: Pause sales or restock
- Click alert to update inventory

---

## 🎨 Color Coding System

```
🟢 GREEN    = Good Status (In Stock, Active, Confirmed)
🟡 YELLOW   = Warning (Low Stock, Pending)
🔴 RED      = Alert (Out of Stock, Failed)
🔵 BLUE     = Info (Processing, Shipped)
🟣 PURPLE   = Primary Action, Featured Items
```

## 📈 Dashboard Insights

### Revenue Trend
- **Calculated**: Sum of all completed orders
- **Shows**: Total ₹ and monthly growth %
- **Use**: Track business health

### Order Growth
- **Calculated**: Count of all orders
- **Shows**: Total orders and growth %
- **Use**: Monitor sales volume

### Conversion Rate
- **Calculated**: (Orders ÷ Users) × 100
- **Shows**: % of users who made purchase
- **Use**: Evaluate marketing effectiveness

### Average Order Value
- **Calculated**: Total Revenue ÷ Total Orders
- **Shows**: ₹ value per order
- **Use**: Optimize pricing strategy
